# Linguagem visual do DOZERO

Derived from the visual reference supplied by the user. Preserve the project's own identity rather than copying a third-party product literally.

## Direction

- Dark, dense tabletop workspace with the canvas/content as the focal plane.
- Near-black navy and blue-green surfaces; emerald green for primary actions and live states.
- Muted borders and shadows; glow is reserved for active, connected or successful states.
- Semantic accent colors distinguish entity types, alerts and tools.
- Compact side navigation and floating panels should remain legible during a session.
- Typography favors fast scanning at small sizes, with display type used sparingly for lore atmosphere.

## Interaction

- Every icon-only action needs an accessible name and visible hover/focus state.
- Primary actions state the result: "Criar cena", "Salvar calendário", "Entrar na mesa".
- Empty states explain the next useful action.
- Loading, offline, synced, saving and error states must be visibly distinct.
- Keep keyboard, touch targets and reduced motion working.

## Restraint

Do not add decorative gradients, glow, motion or glass layers by default. Add them when they reinforce hierarchy or feedback. Avoid visual-only rewrites while a requested functional path is incomplete.
